The Tournament Day Fuel Plan

What to eat and drink from the night before to the last whistle, so your athlete still has legs in the final game. Print it, stick it on the fridge, pack the cooler.

What's inside

  • The night before and game morning. Dinner, bedtime and a breakfast timeline, plus what to do when the first game is at 8 AM.
  • The between-games chart. Under an hour, one to two, two to three, or three-plus: what to eat for every size of gap, and what to skip at the concession stand.
  • The water plan. When to drink all day, the pee check, hot-day rules and the warning signs to watch for.
  • The cooler checklist. Drinks, quick carbs, meals, protein and extras, ready to tick off the night before.
  • The fridge sheet. Fill in your game times and snack windows, plus what to do after the last game.

Questions parents ask

Parents of youth athletes in any sport with multi-game days: soccer, baseball, softball, basketball, volleyball, lacrosse, hockey and swim meets.
