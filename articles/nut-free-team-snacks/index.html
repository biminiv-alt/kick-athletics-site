Nut-free team snacks: easy ideas to share

Many teams now ask snack parents to go nut-free so every kid can share. The good news is that the best team snacks were never nut-based to begin with. Fruit, salty carbs and water do most of the work.

Fruit

  • Orange slices or clementines
  • Grapes (cut for little kids)
  • Bananas
  • Watermelon or apple slices
  • Applesauce pouches

Salty carbs

  • Pretzels
  • Goldfish crackers or plain crackers
  • Popcorn for older kids
  • Rice cakes

Store-bought options

Many granola bars, cereal bars and crackers are made on shared equipment with nuts. Read the label every time, including the "may contain" line, because recipes change. When in doubt, ask the parent of the child with the allergy which brands they trust.

Drinks

Water first. A sports drink can help after a long, hot game. Juice boxes are fine as a treat but not needed.

Tips for the snack parent

  • Check with the coach or the family of the child with the allergy before the season
  • Keep snacks in their original packaging so labels can be checked
  • Skip homemade baked goods unless the family has said they are okay
  • Bring wipes so kids can clean hands before eating
Easy nut-free team snack

A bag of clementines, a big bag of pretzels and a case of water. Cheap, quick and every kid can eat it.
