What to eat after practice

By Kick Athletics · November 23, 2026 · 3 min read

Most families think about food before games. The hour after practice matters too, especially in a week with practice four or five days in a row. Refilling well today means better legs tomorrow.

In the car: an easy snack

If dinner is more than about an hour away, a snack right after practice helps. Carbs plus a little protein.

  • Chocolate milk
  • A banana and a cheese stick
  • A peanut butter sandwich or half bagel
  • Yogurt and granola
  • Trail mix with dried fruit

Dinner: a simple plate

Half the plate carbs (rice, pasta, potatoes, bread), a quarter protein (chicken, beef, fish, eggs, beans), and a quarter fruit or vegetables. Here is roughly how much protein they need.

Drink to catch up

Most kids finish practice a little behind on fluids. Water in the car and with dinner. If their shirt is soaked or there are salt marks, add a little salt to dinner or a sports drink.

When practice ends late

Late practice and a late dinner is fine. Keep it simple and not too heavy, like pasta, a rice bowl, eggs and toast, or a sandwich. A full stomach at 10 pm can hurt sleep, so eat as soon as you get home.

What not to do

  • Skipping food until the next morning
  • Only a sports drink and nothing to eat
  • Fast food every night after practice
This week

Keep chocolate milk and bananas in the car on practice days, and get dinner on the table within an hour or two.
