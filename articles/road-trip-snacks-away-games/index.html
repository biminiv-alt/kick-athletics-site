Road trip snacks for away games

Away games and travel tournaments often mean an hour or more in the car before warmups. That drive is a chance to top off the tank, or to fill up on gas station junk. A small bag packed the night before makes the difference.

What to pack

  • Bananas, clementines and grapes
  • Pretzels, crackers and Goldfish
  • Granola bars or fig bars
  • PB&J or turkey sandwiches, cut in halves
  • String cheese or yogurt tubes in a small cooler bag
  • Water bottles for everyone, plus a sports drink for hot days

Time the eating to the game

  • 3 hours or more before warmups: a real meal before you leave or a sandwich early in the drive.
  • 1 to 2 hours before: a lighter snack like a granola bar and fruit.
  • Last 30 to 60 minutes: something small and easy like a banana or a few pretzels, plus water.

More on timing food before activity.

What to skip

  • Chips, candy and pastries as the main fuel
  • Big fast food meals in the last two hours
  • Energy drinks of any kind
  • Large sodas, which can upset stomachs in warmups

If a drive-through is the only option, there are good picks. Here is how to order fast food before a game.

The drive home

Pack chocolate milk or a sandwich for right after the game. Kids are hungriest in the first hour after playing, and a planned recovery snack beats a random stop. More on what to eat after a game.

Simple road trip bag

Fruit, pretzels, granola bars, half sandwiches, water for everyone, and chocolate milk for the ride home.
