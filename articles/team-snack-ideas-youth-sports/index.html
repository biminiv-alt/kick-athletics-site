Team snack ideas that actually fuel kids

Your name is on the team snack list this week. The easy move is a box of cookies and a case of juice pouches. Kids will be happy for about four minutes. Here is how to bring something they will still be happy with, that also helps them recover.

What a good team snack does

The post-game team snack is really a mini recovery snack. The best ones have:

  • Carbs to start refilling energy
  • Some fluid, either in the food (fruit) or a drink
  • A little protein if it is easy
  • Kid appeal. If they will not eat it, it does not count

Easy, crowd-pleasing ideas

  • Orange slices or clementines
  • Bananas
  • Grapes in individual cups
  • Watermelon slices on warm days
  • Cheese sticks with pretzels or crackers
  • Yogurt tubes (frozen ones thaw just in time)
  • Applesauce pouches
  • Mini bagels
  • Graham crackers or fig bars
  • Single-serve chocolate milk if you can keep it cold

Drinks

Water is the default, and most kids have their own bottle. If you bring drinks, small water bottles or single-serve milk are better than juice pouches and soda. For a normal-length game, kids do not need sports drinks as a team snack.

What to skip

  • Bakery cookies, donuts and cupcakes as the whole snack
  • Chips as the main item
  • Soda and energy drinks
  • Anything with nuts unless you have checked with every family

A treat now and then is part of being a kid. The point is that the team snack does not have to be dessert every week.

Allergies and logistics

  • Ask the coach or team parent about allergies before you shop
  • Individually packed items are easier to hand out and safer for allergies
  • Bring a few extras for siblings and the kid who forgot theirs
  • A small cooler with ice keeps yogurt, cheese and milk safe
  • Bring a trash bag. The other parents will notice

The cheap, easy combo

If you want one answer: a bag of clementines, a box of cheese sticks and a bag of pretzels, split into small bags. Carbs, a little protein, kid-approved, and inexpensive for a whole team.

This week

Share this list with your team's snack coordinator, or bring one fruit and one carb-plus-protein item the next time it is your turn.
