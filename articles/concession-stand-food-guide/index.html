The concession stand: best picks between games

By Kick Athletics · November 27, 2026 · 3 min read

Forgot the cooler, or the tournament ran longer than planned? The concession stand is where most kids end up. Most stands sell a few good options. You just have to look past the candy wall.

Best choices for between games

  • Bananas, apples or orange slices if they have them
  • Pretzels, either a bag or a soft pretzel
  • A plain hot dog or turkey sandwich on a bun
  • Granola bars or cereal bars
  • Peanut butter crackers
  • Water and sports drinks

Okay in small amounts

  • Popcorn: light but salty, so pair it with water
  • A slice of cheese pizza if the next game is 2 or more hours away
  • Chocolate milk after the last game

Better saved for after

  • Nachos with cheese sauce
  • Candy, slushies and big sodas
  • Fried food like fries, mozzarella sticks or chicken baskets
  • Energy drinks: not for kids

Timing matters more than the menu

With less than an hour before the next game, stick to fruit, pretzels and water. With 1 to 2 hours, add a granola bar or half a sandwich. With more than 2 hours, a real meal is fine. Here are the most common between-game mistakes.

Snack bar order between games

A soft pretzel or banana, a bottle of water and a sports drink to share. Under $10 and nothing that will sit heavy in game two.
