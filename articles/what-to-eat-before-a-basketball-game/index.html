What a youth basketball player should eat before a game

Basketball is short bursts over and over: sprint, stop, jump, sprint again. Kids who start under-fueled usually look sharp in the first half and then miss easy shots and get beat down the floor late. Most of that is fixable with timing.

3 to 4 hours before tip-off: the real meal

Mostly carbs, some protein, not too greasy.

  • Pasta with meat sauce or chicken
  • A turkey or chicken sandwich with fruit and pretzels
  • Rice bowl with chicken
  • For a morning game: pancakes or a bagel with eggs and fruit

1 to 2 hours before: a snack

  • A banana or a handful of grapes
  • A granola bar
  • Half a peanut butter sandwich
  • Yogurt

The last 30 minutes

Keep it tiny and only if they are hungry: a few pretzels, orange slices or an applesauce pouch. Then water.

On the bench and at halftime

Gyms get hot, and kids sweat more than parents think. A full water bottle on the bench, a sip at every timeout, and a few orange slices or a little sports drink at halftime in a long or tight game.

What to skip before a basketball game

  • Fast food or fried food in the last few hours
  • A big meal in the hour before warmup
  • Soda and energy drinks
  • Showing up having only had a snack all day

Weekend tournaments

Basketball tournaments often mean two or three games a day in the same gym. Pack the cooler so the gaps are snacks, not concession stand candy. Here is how to handle the gap between games.

Next game

Real meal 3 to 4 hours before tip-off, small snack 1 to 2 hours before, water on the bench, oranges at halftime.
