Junior tennis tournaments: what to eat and drink

Tennis has no game clock. A match can be 45 minutes or two and a half hours, often in full sun, and junior tournaments can mean two or three matches in a day. Fueling has to work for whatever the draw throws at you.

Breakfast before the first match

  • Oatmeal with fruit
  • A bagel with peanut butter and a banana
  • Eggs, toast and juice

Changeovers

Every changeover is a chance to drink and grab a bite. Long matches go better with small, steady fuel.

  • Sips of water at every changeover
  • A sports drink in long matches or heat
  • A few bites of banana
  • Pretzels or a few crackers

Heat and cramping

Long hot matches are where tennis players cramp. Start the day well hydrated, keep sipping, and use a sports drink and salty snacks on hot days. More on cramping and electrolytes.

Between matches

The next match time often moves. Eat a light meal right after the first match, like a sandwich, fruit and water, then keep a snack ready for a 20 minute warning. More on eating between matches.

What goes in the tennis bag

  • Two big water bottles, one frozen
  • Sports drink
  • Bananas
  • Pretzels and granola bars
  • Half sandwiches in a small cooler
  • A towel and a hat
Tournament day

Real breakfast, water at every changeover, sports drink in the heat, a sandwich between matches, and a snack ready for the call.
