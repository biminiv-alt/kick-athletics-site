Two games in one day: what to eat in between

The second game of the day is where tired legs show up. Most of the time it is not fitness. It is what happened, or did not happen, in the gap between games.

Right after game one: refill fast

The first 30 minutes after the whistle are the best time to start refilling. Your athlete does not need a meal yet. They need fluids and some easy carbs, with a little protein.

  • Water plus a sports drink if it was hot or they sweat a lot
  • Chocolate milk
  • A banana or orange slices
  • Pretzels or crackers
  • A granola bar

How long is the gap?

Less than 2 hours

Keep it to snacks. Small and often beats one big plate. A half sandwich, fruit, pretzels and water spread over the gap works better than a burger at the concession stand.

2 to 3 hours

A light meal right after the post-game snack window: a turkey or PB&J sandwich, fruit, crackers, and water. Then a small top-off about 45 minutes before warmup.

More than 3 hours

A real meal is fine, as long as it is not heavy or greasy. Rice or pasta bowls, a wrap, or a sandwich with fruit. Then the same small top-off before warmup.

What makes game two worse

  • Fried food and fast food in a short gap
  • A big pile of candy or soda from the concession stand
  • Nothing at all because they "are not hungry"
  • Forgetting to drink once the game ends

Keep them off their feet

Food is half of it. Shade, a chair, and staying out of the sun between games all help the legs come back for game two.

Pack it before you leave

The gap goes better when the food is already in the cooler. Here is what goes in a tournament food kit.

Next double-header

Pack chocolate milk, fruit, a half sandwich and pretzels for the gap, and set a phone alarm for 45 minutes before the second warmup.
