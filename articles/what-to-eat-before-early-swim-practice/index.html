What to eat before early morning swim practice

By Kick Athletics · November 27, 2026 · 4 min read

Early swim practice is hard on families. Kids are up before 5, not hungry, and heading into an hour or more of hard work. Swimming on an empty stomach can leave them tired and slow, and starving by mid-morning. A small, easy bite before the pool helps a lot.

Quick bites before the pool

Go small and easy to digest. Even a few bites help.

  • A banana
  • A slice of toast with honey or a little peanut butter
  • A granola bar or a few crackers
  • An applesauce pouch
  • A small glass of juice or a sports drink if they cannot eat

If they are not hungry

Start with liquid. A small glass of juice, a smoothie or chocolate milk is easier than solid food at 5 AM. Have them sip it in the car. Most kids get used to eating something small after a couple of weeks.

Swimmers still sweat

Kids sweat in the pool even if they cannot feel it. Send a water bottle to every practice and have them drink during breaks.

After practice: breakfast for real

Right after practice is the time for a real breakfast: eggs and toast, a breakfast sandwich, yogurt with granola and fruit, or oatmeal with milk. If they head straight to school, pack it to eat in the car. More on what to eat after practice.

Plan the night before

A good dinner the night before and a snack bag by the door make early mornings easier. Here is what a good dinner the night before looks like.

Easy 5 AM routine

A banana or juice in the car, water during practice, and a real breakfast with protein right after.
